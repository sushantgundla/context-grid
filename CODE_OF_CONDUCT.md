# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue with the work, not the person.

## What that means here

This project is about disagreeing productively — most of its content is a record of a claim
being checked and found wrong. That only works if being wrong is cheap.

**Expected:**

- Criticise the code, the number, the claim. Not the person who wrote it.
- Assume the other person had a reason. Ask what it was before concluding they were careless.
- Say "I was wrong" plainly when you are. It costs nothing here and it saves everyone time.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no. A project that accepts everything means nothing.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests, commits or private mail.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments about anyone's identity — including but not limited to
  age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race,
  religion, or sexual orientation.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption: reopening settled arguments to wear people down, or brigading a thread.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens — the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space
where someone is representing it.

## Enforcement

Report to **gundla.sushant@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept in
confidence, including from the person reported.

Responses run from a private word, through having a comment removed, to being blocked from the
project. Which one depends on severity and on whether it has happened before. If you think a
decision was wrong, say so in reply — that is a fair thing to challenge.

The maintainer is held to this document too. There is only one right now, which makes it awkward
to report him to himself; if that ever matters to you, say so publicly in an issue rather than
staying quiet.
